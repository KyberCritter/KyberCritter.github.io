I've decided to shut down the Pygskin website, effective immediately. The website is no longer accessible, but the [source code](https://github.com/KyberCritter/Pygskin-Webapp) is still available on GitHub. To read more about what the project was, read the [project page](/projects/pygskin/).

## Reasoning

After facing technical difficulties with DigitalOcean and weighing the costs, I decided against renewing the service. Although I will still control the Pygskin domain until early next year, it will simply redirect to the homepage of this website. I will not renew the domain registration next year.

I have something special in the works that might excite my fellow retro gaming enthusiasts. Stay tuned for more details.
